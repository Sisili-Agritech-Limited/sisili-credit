Our assessment approach

What we look at instead of collateral.

A person reviews your application, not a black box. We tell you which signals we used — and if the answer is no, we tell you what would change it.

1
What you share
Business activity Savings behaviour Group participation Repayment history Transaction patterns Agricultural activity Income patterns References
Which of these apply depends on the product and what you have. We never require information you don't hold.
