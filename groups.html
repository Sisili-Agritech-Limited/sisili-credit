Why groups get further with us

Collective financial behaviour
Regular contributions from many members are a stronger signal than one person's payslip.
Shared accountability
Groups police repayment better than lenders do, because the relationships outlast the loan.
Structures that already work
We don't ask you to form a new committee. We work with the officers you already elected.
Community standing
A group with a reputation in its area has something a credit file can't capture.
A record members can carry
Individual contribution history becomes a personal profile for future borrowing.

What we ask of a group

Requirements are being finalised with our legal and credit teams. The direction is below; the final list will be published before we lend.

A minimum number of members At least 5 members
A contribution history we can see At least 6 months
Elected officers we can contact Chair, treasurer, secretary
Group registration certificate Where applicable
Members' ID details For verification
One member's difficulty does not become the whole group's punishment. Where a group guarantee applies, it will be stated in plain language in the offer document, with the exact consequence spelled out before anyone signs.
