About

Credit is not scarce in Kenya. Fair credit is.

Millions of people can get a loan on their phone in ninety seconds. Far fewer can get one priced fairly, sized sensibly, and built around how their money actually arrives. Sisili Credit exists for the second thing.

Mission

To make productive credit ordinary for the farmers, traders and groups who carry Kenya's economy — priced honestly and sized to what they can repay.

Vision

A financial system where belonging to a group that saves together counts for as much as owning land.

How we work

Lend less than you asked for
If the numbers don't support the amount, we say so. An oversized loan helps nobody twice.
Price it once, publish it
One published price per product. No negotiated rates, no hidden premium for the least informed borrower.
Human review, always
A person makes every credit decision and signs their name to the reason.
Meet people where they are
WhatsApp, a phone call, or an officer in the field. Never a requirement to own a smartphone.
Build the record together
Every interaction adds to a profile the customer owns and can take elsewhere.
Say what we don't know
Where the answer isn't decided, this website says so rather than inventing something reassuring.
