How it works

From first message to money, in six steps.

You can do all of it on WhatsApp. If you'd rather sit with someone, an officer will meet your group.

01

Register

Send us a message and answer three questions: your name, your ID number, and what you do. No documents to upload, no branch visit.

Takes
About two minutes, on WhatsApp or with a credit officer.
02

Build your financial profile

Tell us how the money comes in — daily takings, harvest seasons, group contributions. Share records if you keep them; if you don't, we'll build the picture with you.

You'll need
Nothing formal. Receipts, an M-Pesa statement or a group ledger all help, but none is required to start.
03

Apply for credit

Pick the product and the amount. Before you agree to anything, we show you the total you will repay, the instalments, and the dates.

You will see
Amount received, all fees, total repayable and every due date — on one screen, before you commit.
04

Get assessed

A credit officer reviews your application against the signals you shared. Not an algorithm, and not a rubber stamp.

Either way
You get a written reason. If it's a no, we tell you specifically what would make it a yes.
05

Receive financing

Approved money goes to your mobile money account, or to the group account for group credit. Input financing can be paid directly to a supplier if you prefer.

Timing
To be confirmed at launch. We will publish a target and report against it.
06

Repay and build your record

Pay on the dates agreed. Pay early at no extra cost. Every settled loan raises your limit and improves the terms on the next one.

If things go wrong
Tell us before the date. Restructuring an instalment is normal; hiding from it is expensive.

No smartphone? No problem.

The same six steps run over a phone call or in person with a credit officer. Nothing about this service requires an app.
