Questions

Straight answers, or none at all.

Where the business decision isn't made yet, we say so instead of writing something vague.

Who can apply?
Farmers, market traders, small business owners and organised groups in Kenya. You need an ID and a way we can reach you. You do not need a bank account or a payslip.
Do I need collateral?
No. We assess you on your economic activity, savings behaviour and group standing. Some larger facilities may have conditions, which will always be stated in the offer document before you sign.
Can a group apply?
Yes — group credit is central to what we do. A chama, youth group, cooperative or farmer group can borrow as one, using its savings and repayment record.
How do I apply?
Send a WhatsApp message, call us, or speak to a credit officer in the field. There is no form to download and no branch to visit.
What does it cost?
Pricing is being finalised. We will publish one clear price per product before we begin lending, and the total repayable will always be shown before you agree to anything. [Content placeholder — awaiting product decision.]
How much can I borrow?
Limits by product are still being set. They will depend on your activity, your record with us, and — for groups — your collective savings. [Content placeholder — awaiting product decision.]
How long does approval take?
A credit officer reviews every application. We will publish a target turnaround time at launch and report our actual performance against it. [Content placeholder.]
How is repayment handled?
Through mobile money on the dates in your schedule. Paying early costs nothing extra. Group loans are repaid from the group account.
What if I miss a payment?
Contact us before the due date and we will work out a revised schedule. A late fee may apply — it will be stated in your offer document. Missing payments affects your standing with us.
How are my details protected?
Your information is used to assess your application and manage your account, nothing else. We do not sell customer data. Our full privacy policy will be published before launch.
Are you licensed?
We are a registered Kenyan entity and our digital credit provider licence application is in progress with the Central Bank of Kenya. We are not yet lending.
How do I reach a person?
WhatsApp is fastest. You can also call the number on our contact page, weekdays 8am to 6pm and Saturdays 9am to 1pm.
